## 2. Addressing the "Gaps" (How to handle weaknesses)

**The JD mentions "VB" (Visual Basic):** 
Many traditional Japanese manufacturing companies still use older legacy software written in Visual Basic. 
*   **Your Strategy:** Do not say you don't know VB or that it is bad. Instead, say: *"While my primary project uses modern Python and TypeScript, I am a very fast learner. I am completely willing to learn and maintain your existing Visual Basic systems, and eventually help the company modernize them into Python when the time is right."*

**The JD mentions "Management/updating of the company website":**
*   **Your Strategy:** Mention that you built your frontend using React.js and Vite. Managing a standard corporate website will be incredibly easy for you given your advanced web development skills.

## 3. Highlighting the "Soft Skills" (Crucial for Japanese Companies)

The JD emphasizes behavioral traits heavily:
> *"Someone who does not give up easily... stay positive and take it as a challenge... think and act independently... proactive research."*

**Your Strategy:** Use your project as the ultimate proof of this!

1.  **Bring the Project to the Interview:** If the interview is offline in Bangalore, bring a tablet or a laptop.
