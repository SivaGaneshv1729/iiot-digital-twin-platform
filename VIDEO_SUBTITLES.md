# 🎬 Video Subtitles Script (for Silent Demo Video)

Since your video currently has no audio, you can add these text subtitles (using a free tool like **CapCut**, **Premiere Pro**, or **Clipchamp**) to explain what is happening on the screen to the viewer. 

You can pace these subtitles based on how fast your video moves.

---

**Subtitle 3:** Watch as real-time machine data flows in instantly.

---

### 💡 Tip on how to add these subtitles to your video easily:
1. **Download CapCut** (It is free and very popular).
2. Import your silent video.
3. Click on **Text** -> **Add Text**.
4. Copy and paste the subtitles above one by one.
5. Drag the edges of the text blocks on the timeline to match the timing of what is happening in your video.
6. Choose a highly readable font (like **Inter** or **Roboto**) with a black shadow or black background so it stands out against the 3D map!
